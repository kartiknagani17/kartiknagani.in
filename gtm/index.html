How a campaign runs

Same steps every time. The work is in doing each one properly.

  1. Pick the market

    Who are we going after, where are they, and why would they care?

  2. Build the list

    Scrape Google Maps or pull from Apollo, then cut anyone who doesn't fit.

  3. Enrich it

    Find the right person, a working email and a real reason to reach out.

  4. Write and send

    A short first email and a few follow-ups, sent through Instantly.

  5. Handle replies

    Sort the replies, answer interested people fast and hand booked calls to the client.
